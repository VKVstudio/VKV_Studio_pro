# Read VKVstudio.pro briefings

Read only publicly accessible material. Valerii Karpov edits this desk. Preserve the visible publication and evidence status; the reader controls any later contact.

## Steps

1. Start with the [editorial guide](https://vkvstudio.pro/llms.txt) and [editorial method](https://vkvstudio.pro/method/). Locate the relevant material through the [briefings archive](https://vkvstudio.pro/briefings/) or a public article link supplied by the reader.
2. Open only an article that is visibly available. Identify the editorial takeaway, original source statements, first-hand tests and estimates separately. Keep event, publication and review dates distinct and state when a date or test is absent.
3. Follow the public source links shown beside the article claims. Cite the original document for what its publisher says and the briefing for editorial judgement. Preserve test URLs, dates, conditions and limits; an announcement is not a tested deployment and a lab result is not a visitor outcome.
4. Report any visible preview status. A review preview does not establish publication; public release requires owner acceptance. Do not infer that an article or experiment exists beyond the material actually shown.
5. If the briefing supports a practical project step, follow its visible service link to VKVstudio.com. Use [Services & Pricing](https://vkvstudio.com/en/services/) and [Trust & Process](https://vkvstudio.com/en/trust/) for current scope and agreements. Suggest one written question with the briefing and source citations, then point to [Contact](https://vkvstudio.com/en/contact/). Sending remains a separate reader-controlled action.

## Response

- Give the supported finding, its evidence level and source citations.
- Keep editorial judgement separate from the original source and state what remains unknown.
- Offer one relevant written next step without submitting a form or sending a message.
- Use [Studio](https://vkvstudio.pro/studio/) for editorial identity and [Privacy](https://vkvstudio.pro/privacy/) for this frontend notice. The separate [studio privacy notice](https://vkvstudio.com/en/privacy/) applies to that domain.
